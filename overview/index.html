📋 Overview

Portionist is a smart, AI-powered web application that helps you plan meals, track nutrition, and reach your health goals. Discover recipes, get personalized suggestions, and monitor your daily food intake - all in one easy-to-use platform.

✅ Live Now: Web app currently available at portionist.netlify.app. Mobile apps for iOS and Android coming soon!

🎯 What Can You Do?

  • Get Personalized Meals: Receive recipe suggestions tailored to your weight goals and favorite cuisines
  • Find Recipes: Search thousands of recipes with complete nutrition information for each meal
  • Track Your Meals: Log what you eat throughout the day and see your progress
  • AI Recipe Generator: Create custom recipes using smart AI technology based on ingredients you have
  • Access Anywhere: Use it on your computer through any web browser. Mobile apps coming soon!

🔒 Your Privacy & Data

What Information We Store

Portionist keeps your data safe and secure. Here's what we save to make your experience personalized and helpful:

Account Information

  • Email Address: For logging in and account recovery
  • Password: Encrypted and securely stored
  • Full Name: To personalize your experience

Your Health Goals

  • Current & Target Weight: To provide appropriate meal suggestions
  • Age: Helps calculate nutrition needs
  • Favorite Cuisines: To recommend recipes you'll enjoy

Saved Recipes

  • Favorite Recipes: Your bookmarked meals with nutrition info
  • Recipe Images: Photos of dishes you've saved

Meal History

  • Logged Meals: What you ate and when
  • Nutrition Totals: Daily protein, carbs, fat, and calories
  • Meal Types: Whether it was breakfast, lunch, dinner, or a snack
✅ Your Privacy Matters: We never sell your personal information or share it with third parties. Your data is encrypted and stored securely on protected cloud servers.
